### [Roqer](https://github.com/S4US/Roqer)

Tell it what you want, and it builds it in your place, using the ChatGPT or Claude subscription you already have.

- 🧠 **Bring your own model**: ChatGPT via Codex, Claude via Claude Code, or any OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible endpoint, local models included
- 🧱 **Works in your place**: reads and edits instances, properties and scripts, and won't overwrite changes you made after it looked
- 🎨 **Models in Blender**: curved shapes, props and vehicle bodies that parts can't make, checked with a preview render and uploaded to your place
- 🎮 **Tests its own work**: solo and multi-client playtests, output logs, screenshots and profiling
- 🔌 **Standalone MCP server**: use the Studio tools from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any MCP client
- 🔒 **Fully local**: no account, no Roqer server, chats stay on your machine
